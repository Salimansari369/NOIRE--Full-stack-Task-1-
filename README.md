> *"A whisper in the dark, an unforgettable trail. NOIRÉ captures the enigmatic essence of the midnight hour."*

**NOIRÉ** is a high-end, luxury editorial landing page for an artisanal extrait de parfum house. Designed with a dark mode aesthetic, sophisticated typography (`Cormorant Garamond` & `Poppins`), subtle atmospheric ambient glow effects, and modern CSS layout architectures.

---

## ✨ Features & Visual Highlights

* **Atmospheric Ambient Visuals:** Dynamic background radial gradients that create subtle, ethereal lighting effects.
* **Refined Typography & Spacing:** Editorial styling using Cormorant Garamond serif headers paired with clean geometric Poppins body typography.
* **Curated Collection Showcase:** Interactive card layouts presenting signature flacons, concentration profiles, and scent accords.
* **Artisanal Storytelling & Features:** Sections detailing cold extraction, sustainable sourcing, and master perfumer curation.
* **Bespoke Concierge & Newsletter:** High-fashion styling for VIP newsletter subscription and customer inquiry forms.
* **Fully Responsive:** Fluid layouts built with Flexbox and CSS Grid, including an accessible mobile drawer navigation.

---

## 🏛️ Page Sections

1. **Header & Navigation:** Sticky luxury brand bar with smooth scrolling triggers and mobile menu toggle.
2. **Hero Section:** High-impact campaign statement with call-to-action buttons (*"Explore Scent"* / *"The Archive"*).
3. **The Collection (`#collection`):** Product catalog highlighting extrait concentrations and scent notes.
4. **Artisanal Craftsmanship (`#features`):** Highlight cards focusing on rare botanical extracts, maceration, and bespoke bottling.
5. **Brand Heritage & Story (`#story`):** The narrative of midnight distillation and atelier philosophy.
6. **Bespoke Services (`#services`):** Private consultations, monogramming, and concierge delivery.
7. **Private Registry / Contact (`#contact`):** Interactive VIP mailing subscription.
8. **Footer:** Quick links, legal policies, and social media channels.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Implementation Details

* **Markup:** Semantic HTML5 (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`) with accessibility attributes (`aria-hidden`, `aria-label`).
* **Styling:** Custom Vanilla CSS3 using custom properties (`:root` design tokens), CSS Grid, Flexbox, backdrop filters, and subtle transition animations.
* **Iconography:** FontAwesome 6.5.2 Vector Icons.
* **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Cormorant Garamond` & `Poppins`).
